They do not authorize orders, change inputs, supply market data or implement a historical replay adapter. `EntryRiskPolicyApproved()` remains false; `FutureCostsKnown()` remains unavailable and independently vetoes candidates. Normal-terminal execution and optimization remain refused. The API path is still an unconditional veto. A successful compile is evidence of compilation only; the current Windows evidence is tracked separately in `docs/VALIDATION_RESULTS.md` and `tests/windows/evidence/`.

The account deposit, currency, leverage, broker, broker symbol and acceptable economic drawdown for **this project are unconfirmed**. Do not import the VTMarkets / 2,000 / 500:1 / 30% choices from WeiweiFX or another project. Existing source limits, including 75 account-currency daily loss, 35 basket loss, 5% fixed-initial-equity drawdown, 0.25% initial-risk ceiling and the approved 2x closed-M5-ATR protective-stop rule, remain unchanged. These are code controls, not a user-approved backtest account specification or a guaranteed loss ceiling.

## 2. Data package and custom-symbol import

Candidate search years are **2018–2025**; actual coverage, licensing and availability are unknown. No dataset, provider, download or complete eight-year sample is promised. An unauthenticated, unlicensed, unexplained or unverifiable source is `NOT_VALIDATED`; its output may be inspected as diagnostics but cannot pass the historical experiment gate or be described as broker-realistic performance.

Retain immutable raw bytes and a separately hashed normalized package, provider/instrument identifiers, acquisition time, version, license/use permission, source URLs or references, transformation commit and complete import/export logs. Build a coverage report by UTC day, session and year, with row counts, duplicate/out-of-order events, spread distribution, timestamp resolution, missing-side records, missing intervals, provider outages and rejected records. Do not interpolate prices or silently delete gaps/spikes to improve results. Review suspect observations against original evidence and retain the rejection ledger.

Proposed normalized tick schema (an interchange format, **not** a claim about MT5's GUI CSV layout):

| Field | Required rule |
|---|---|
| `source_id`, `instrument_id`, `sequence` | Immutable provenance and stable tie-order for identical millisecond timestamps. Do not deduplicate solely on timestamp. |
| `time_utc_msc`, `time_utc` | Positive Unix milliseconds and matching exact UTC rendering; preserve original resolution. Neither may be zero. |
| `bid`, `ask` | Finite positive executable two-sided quotes; ask >= bid. Preserve variable spread. A last-only or mid-only series is not an executable CFD quote stream. |
| `bid_updated`, `ask_updated`, `bid_observed_utc_msc`, `ask_observed_utc_msc` | Preserve side-change and source-age information. Carry-forward only when the source protocol explicitly supplies quote-state updates; no synthetic reconstruction of an unknown side. A quote-age acceptance policy is required before orders. |
| `last`, `volume`, `volume_real`, source flags | Optional if absent from the original OTC quote source; never invent trade volume or treat it as executable bid/ask. Map supported MT5 fields explicitly and retain original flags externally. |

Use a distinct offline custom symbol whose name identifies this research dataset, with no broker account or online feed. The chart/test symbol and the test-only `InpSymbol` value must be identical; the matching suffix is part of the reviewed `.set`, not a committed-default change. Clone no current broker contract without a versioned specification review. Copy all relevant custom-symbol properties explicitly, export them and hash them. A custom symbol does not prove data authenticity or broker equivalence.

The importer is **not yet implemented**. Its future review must check ordered nonzero millisecond timestamps and exact counts/hashes, then read back imported ticks and derived M1/M5/H1 bars to compare times, quotes and boundaries against the normalized package. MetaQuotes documents ordered `MqlTick` arrays and special handling of zero timestamps, so zero-time rows must be rejected before import. Original bid/ask flags and age metadata must remain in the external archive even if the terminal normalizes its stored flags. See [CustomTicksAdd](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/customsymbols/customticksadd), [CustomTicksReplace](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/customsymbols/customticksreplace) and [MqlTick](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/structures/mqltick).

All testing timestamps must represent **UTC**, including server/tester simulated time. `TimeGMT()` in the tester is not proof of UTC. Convert documented provider timezone/DST once, preserve raw times and conversion rules, reject ambiguous/nonexistent local timestamps until resolved, then compare representative sessions and DST/weekend boundaries. Provision the two-line local `anticipatory_test_scope.txt` exactly as `MQL5/STAGE2.md` requires. Each independent run has a fresh deliberate ID; restart/fault continuations retain their ID and evidence. Never delete a halted scope to get a clean result.

## 3. Historical instrument, account and cost evidence

The manifest must identify effective-dated contract regimes. If a historical specification cannot be authenticated, label a single fixed specification as a synthetic scenario and do not claim it reproduces the broker historically. Record at least:

- Symbol exact name, base/profit/margin currencies, contract size, digits, point, tick size, tick-value profit/loss and calculation mode. Validate price-to-account-currency `OrderCalcProfit` examples at multiple dates/prices, including historical FX conversion quotes where required.
- Minimum/maximum/step volume and limit, supported order/SL and fill policies, stops/freeze levels, execution mode, trading/quote sessions, holiday schedules and changes over time. Current code supports hedging account mode only; netting/exchange accounting is blocked.
- Deposit/currency, leverage and effective margin rules, initial/maintenance/hedged margin, margin-call/stop-out modes/levels and cash-flow policy. No external positions, deposits, withdrawals or other EAs may contaminate a dedicated account-wide-equity run.
- Commission and separate fees: currency, per-side versus round-trip, per-lot versus per-deal/notional, entry/exit timing, volume tiers, minimums, rounding, historical changes and cash attribution. Model both sides exactly once; use neither unspecified zero costs nor today's account settings as an authenticated historical schedule. Daily/monthly balance charges and unallocated fees are unsupported by the current ledger; they block broker-representative orders until separately reviewed attribution exists.
- Swap: long/short rates, units/currency/conversion, rollover clock/DST, triple-day rule, holidays, effective dates and overnight duration. The existing profitable-only 180-minute exit is **not** a maximum duration for a losing basket. A future-cost bound must address the permitted holding horizon; if no finite credible bound exists, keep the veto rather than inventing a horizon or adding an unapproved time stop.

Historical actual charges and prospective authorization bounds are different evidence. A verified realized-fee ledger does not prove the future commission/fee/swap bound. Its authorized/verified/known flags, currency, expiry and provenance must be satisfied by reviewed data and implementation; they currently are not. Stop gaps and slippage can exceed modeled loss regardless of those flags.

Use archived variable bid/ask spread as the baseline, without charging the same spread again as a cash fee. For stress runs, transform quotes reproducibly (for example a proposed 2x spread around the unchanged mid) and store a new dataset hash; such transformations are assumptions, not observed prices. Keep side ages and valid ordering. Freeze a cost/delay/slippage scenario matrix before reading holdout results; all magnitudes and whether they are empirical or synthetic must be reviewed.

Tester execution delay is not a universal liquidity/partial-fill/slippage model. Start with a deterministic fixed-delay scenario whose value is currently unknown; record limitations rather than claiming that it simulates broker behavior. Keep any random-delay scenario separately labeled and require repeatability evidence before comparing runs. Gap-through-SL, rejected orders, acknowledgement loss and partial fills need native fault tests in addition to price-history runs. MetaQuotes documents real-tick mode, customizable symbol/account commissions and execution-delay settings; capture the actual selected settings and resulting charges. See [Strategy Testing](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/testing) and [Real and Generated Ticks](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/tick_generation).

## 4. Signals, macro vintages and AI replay

Use closed M5 signals and H1 indicators available at the first eligible tick after close; never backfill a later H1 result into earlier M5 bars. Level bars exclude the candidate bar. Record missing/unsynchronized history and veto reasons, with finite OHLC/buffer validation. Preload sufficient earlier observations for indicator convergence without scoring that warm-up. Its exact horizon/convergence criterion is an unresolved manifest gate, not an arbitrary profitable warm-up selection.

A proposed first performance variant is one layer, AI disabled and macro disabled, explicitly labeled **rules-only/no-macro ablation**. Its test-only settings require review and do not change the committed defaults. Repeated deterministic runs use separate deliberately provisioned state scopes and `Optimization=disabled`; current EA refuses MT5 optimization. Two/three-layer or alternate-parameter variants require separate approval after baseline safety review.

Macro-enabled historical evaluation requires an unimplemented as-of replay adapter for CSV2 source metadata. Archive DXY/nominal-US10Y/real-yield instrument and unit/window, original observation, publication, reception/availability, assembly time and the actual then-available vintage. Preserve news-calendar versions, scheduled event/revision times, importance rules, embargo intervals, original observation and coverage expiry. At decision time, join only records actually available then, validate each original age and calendar coverage, and veto missing/expired evidence. A revised series carrying an old observation date still leaks if its revision became available later. Today's CSV and a daily series rebadged as a fresh 15-minute observation are invalid substitutes.

For each numeric source require `observed_at <= published_at <= received_at <= assembled_at <= decision_at`, with every original numeric observation and assembled snapshot at most 900 seconds old at consumption, as the existing policy requires. Calendar observation must precede assembly and its coverage expiry must include the decision; unknown calendar coverage blocks rather than meaning no news. Calendar publication/reception evidence must likewise establish availability before decision. Preserve scheduled event time separately: a future scheduled event can be known in advance, but its later released outcome cannot be joined early. This plan introduces no new news-age threshold or news embargo duration.

No live LLM may be called from a historical run. AI-filter comparison remains `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` until a reviewed replay procedure exists. It would require archived decisions demonstrably available at the historical instant, or a clearly separated reproducible offline research-model experiment with frozen weights/prompt/features and an honest model-training-data leakage assessment. A model released after the test period is not contemporaneous historical evidence. Correlate signal/request/response IDs and availability clocks, retain vetoes, and compare identical candidate streams and costs. Do not call model confidence a calibrated probability.
